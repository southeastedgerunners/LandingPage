Why we plant
by the moon

It's an old Appalachian habit. We like the rhythm it gives the week. We're Juniper and Wes, and we grow on 9 acres in Bell Hollow without sprays.

phasewhat we do
New moon Sow leafy greens and lettuce.
Waxing moon Plant crops that fruit above ground, like beans, squash, and tomatoes.
Full moon Transplant seedlings and harvest herbs.
Waning moon Plant roots and garlic, weed, and rest the beds.

December
by the moon

We sort the days by the moon. On root days we plant garlic and dig roots. On leaf days we cut kale and greens. On fruit days we sort the stored squash, and on herb days we cut and dry herbs.
