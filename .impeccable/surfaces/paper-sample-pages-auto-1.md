# Redbud Auto Works · shop manual

Scope: Paper page "Auto 1 · Redbud Auto Works (shop manual)" (pageId p-1-0). Persuade mode. Sample landing page for a fictional business, shown by SouthEast EdgeRunners to prospective clients.

Audience: a driver in Southeastern Kentucky with a grinding brake or a check-engine light, reading on a phone, worried about being overcharged. Job: trust this shop enough to book. Action: book a drop-off (working form with day slots) or call.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the shop's own repair manual. It shows you the worn part and the fix before you pay. Refuses the category default of a mechanic photo, a service icon grid and a review carousel.

OWN-WORLD: White manual paper #FFFFFF, black line ink #111111, one safety-orange spot color #FF5A1F used only for callout numbers, the active part, the active step and the primary button. Shading is black hatching or 50% halftone dots, never gray fills. Line drawings in 1.5px black strokes with numbered callout circles and leader lines. Spec tables with hairline rules and tabular figures. Square corners everywhere. A condensed industrial grotesk for headings, a plain readable sans for body.
Raise from the hand-drawn zine challenger: callout leader lines draw themselves in (show them drawn in the static comp).
Raise from the Swiss grid challenger: one master grid (4 columns on phone, 12 on desktop) governs every section, and sizes step in grid units.

STORY: The visitor sees a real brake job drawn and explained, sees typical times and starting prices, learns the shop texts a photo of the worn part before any work, and books a drop-off.

FIRST VIEWPORT (390x844): Status bar. A top rule with the wordmark REDBUD AUTO WORKS left and a "Call" text link right. Headline about 40px condensed: "We show you the worn part before we replace it." An exploded disc-brake drawing, full width, about 280px tall, with five numbered callouts (1 rotor, 2 caliper, 3 brake pads, 4 caliper bracket, 5 slide pins). Callout 3 is the active part, in orange. A two-column legend under the drawing. A full-width orange "Book a drop-off" button and a secondary "Call (606) 555-0142".

## Design notes (as built, 2026-11-27)

Status: finish review ship after one fix round. Paper artboards "Phone · 390" and "Desktop · 1440" on page p-1-0.

- Type: Barlow Condensed (600, 700) for headings, wordmark, callout numbers and spec labels. Public Sans for body, table cells and inputs, with tabular figures in the price column.
- Color: white #FFFFFF, ink #111111, safety orange #FF5A1F. Text on orange is black (about 6:1); white on this orange fails at 3.1:1. The selected drop-off day is black, keeping orange for callouts, the active part and the primary button.
- Grid: phone 4 columns of 80px, 10px gutters, 20px margins. Desktop 12 columns of 86px, 24px gutters, 72px margins.
- The brake drawing (Fig. 1) and the pad-wear drawing (Fig. 2) are generated by brake.py (scratchpad, not durable) and placed as SVG fills that Paper stores in the file's own assets. Callout numbers are editable HTML overlays. The rotor friction ring carries a 50% halftone and the caliper front face is hatched.
- Changes from the content list: the price column header is PRICE with "from" wording per row. Invented claims were removed in review.
