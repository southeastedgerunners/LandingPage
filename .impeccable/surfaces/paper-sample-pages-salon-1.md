# Ten Tips Nail Studio · press-on blister card

Scope: Paper page "Salon 1 · Ten Tips Nail Studio (press-on card)" (pageId p-5-0). Persuade mode. Sample landing page for a fictional business, shown by SouthEast EdgeRunners to prospective clients.

Audience: women in their late teens to thirties in a small Kentucky town, booking a set for prom, a wedding or the weekend from an Instagram link on their phone. Job: love a set and see an open time. Action: pick an open time slot and book (working slot picker in the first viewport).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a press-on nail retail card. The clear blister window shows the set, and the back of the card says what's inside and how to book. Refuses the category default of blush and beige, a thin serif and soft photos of hands.

OWN-WORLD: Cherry-lacquer cardstock #A8102B drenches the page. Chrome foil reads as flat silver #D8DBE0 shapes with one crisp white highlight stroke (no gradient text). Black #111 and white #FFF type. The blister is a large rounded rectangle of clear plastic: a slightly lighter cherry inside, a 2px white highlight stroke along one edge and a glint. A euro-slot hang hole punched through the top center of the card. Packaging grammar: condensed caps info lines (CONTENTS, DIRECTIONS, WARNING), a foil starburst badge, a barcode at the bottom. The nails are drawn SVG almond and square tips, each with its own art (chrome cherry, french tip, tiny stars, tortoiseshell, milky pink, black cat-eye, gold flake, checkerboard, polka dot, solid red). A chunky retail display face with real character, and a clean condensed sans for packaging copy.
Degraded roll: no challengers were dealt, so no raises.

STORY: The visitor sees a set they want inside the window, sees prices and how long a set lasts, sees today's open times, and books one.

FIRST VIEWPORT (390x844): Status bar. The hang tab with the punched euro hole and the wordmark TEN TIPS NAIL STUDIO. Headline: "Fresh set. Lasts three weeks." The blister window holding 10 tips in two rows of five, the set name "The Cherry Pick · gel set · $55" on a silver foil strip. A foil starburst "Walk-ins Thu to Sat". A row of open-time chips for today (2:30, 3:15, 5:00) with one selected, and a full-width black "Book 3:15 today" button.

- Name: Ten Tips Nail Studio. 31 Main Street, Suite B, Pine Fork, KY (fictional town). (606) 555-0163. Instagram @tentips.nails (fictional).
- Hours: Tue to Sat 10 to 7. Walk-ins Thursday to Saturday.
- "Contents" (services · time · price): Gel manicure · 45 min · $40. Gel-X extensions · 90 min · $65. Classic manicure · 30 min · $28. Spa pedicure · 50 min · $45. Nail art · per nail · $3 to $10. Soak-off and fresh coat · 30 min · $20.
- "Directions": 1 Pick a set or bring a picture. 2 Pick a time. 3 Come in with bare nails, or we'll soak off the old ones. 4 Leave with a set that lasts about three weeks.
- "Warning: may cause compliments." Fine for the voice, use it once.
- Booking block further down: day tabs for this week, open times per day, a selected service, name and phone fields, "Book it".

## Design notes (as built, 2026-11-27)

Status: finish review ship after one fix round. Paper artboards "Phone · 390" and "Desktop · 1440" on page p-5-0.

- Type: Shrikhand for the wordmark, headlines, set names and buttons. Sofia Sans Extra Condensed (700 to 900) for packaging info lines, prices and chips. Sofia Sans Condensed (500 to 600) for body at 15 to 17px.
- Color (sRGB canvas values): cherry ground #A8102B, blister interior #BC2741, desktop outer margin #7E0A1F, foil silver #D8DBE0 with a white top highlight, black #111 only on silver or white, white text on cherry (about 7.6:1).
- Changes from the contract above: the window strip reads "Ten tips, ten arts · GEL SETS FROM $50" (the window shows a ten-art sampler, not The Cherry Pick). The headline "Fresh set. Lasts three weeks." is kept on purpose as packaging voice. The numbers 0 to 9 under the tips are press-on sizes.
- Card back: Contents is a white printed panel, Directions a black printed panel ending with the warning, Book a set has a perforated top and one silver tag. "Lot 1127 / Made in Pine Fork, KY" sits beside the barcode. Desktop shows a die-cut card outline with a hang tab.
